# Vault-Tec-Remasterd

This pack is a heavily curated modpack dedicaated to the surviavlist player, here its less about questing and story and more about staying alive long enough to see your player thrive and grow
questing is optional and not enforced here

our recommended specs:

we acheived 45-70fps around diamond city on:
- i7-13650H
- 4050 6gb
- 32gb ddr4 32000mhz

this should be the baseline for the pack and may grow in mods which means it may not be suitable for low end pc's take this at your own
