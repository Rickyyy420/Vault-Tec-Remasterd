# Vault-Tec-Remasterd

This pack is a heavily curated modpack dedicated to the **survivalist player**.  
Here, it’s less about traditional questing and hand-holding storylines, and more about staying alive long enough to see your character **adapt, struggle, and eventually thrive**.

Questing exists, but it is **optional and never enforced**.

---

## Lets begin with requirements — make sure to have **ALL** of the below:

- 1 - **All DLCs** for base Fallout 4 and a **clean install** of the game  
  *(I use Steam, so I can’t account for other platforms)*  
  - Game must be updated to the **newest version**
  - Must be launched **at least once** (Yes, that includes NG / AE)
- 2 - **Fallout 4 Creation Kit** installed and launched **at least once**
- 3 - **250GB of free disk space**  
  *(Preferably not your OS / C: drive, but it will work)*  
  → *subject to change*
---

## our recommended specs:

We achieved **45–70 FPS** (average ~65–75) in most areas, including around Diamond City, on the following system:

- i7-13650H
- RTX 4050 (6GB)
- 32GB DDR4 @ 3200MHz

If you struggle to run this pack, it is fairly easy to disable some of the **texture / ENB / foliage** mods.  
I have also included a **"*potato pc*"** profile which:

- Disables all 2K / 4K textures
- Disables all Vivid Fallout modules
- Disables the majority of foliage mods

*(This is a newer profile and not entirely optimized yet, but it is worth trying for lower-spec PCs.)*

> *This should be considered the baseline for the pack.*  
> As the modlist grows, it **may not remain suitable for low-end PCs**.  
> Use at your own discretion.

# Setup/Install:

In this section, we will walk you through the setup and installation of the modpack.  
The process itself is very straightforward and will only become simpler as updates continue.

⚠️ **It is extremely important that you follow ALL steps exactly as shown.**  
This pack currently contains **~500 mods** in its **ALPHA** phase and will continue to grow.

- Mods may be **removed or swapped** at any point during future updates
- Core gameplay mods such as **MAIM** and **Uneducated Shooter** are expected to remain throughout development
- Most major gameplay changes are already **set in stone**, even at this early stage

---

## Getting started installing:

- 1 - First things first:  
  If you already have Fallout 4 and the Creation Kit installed, you will need **Wabbajack**.  
  Head over to https://www.wabbajack.org/ and download Wabbajack.  
  Place `Wabbajack.exe` inside its **own folder** on any drive  
  *(Example: `D:\Wabbajack`)* and launch it.

- 2 - Next, head over to Nexus Mods and download the **Vault-Tec-Remasterd** zip file.  
  It will likely contain **two files**:
  - a `.json`
  - a `.wabbajack`

  LINK: https://www.nexusmods.com/fallout4/mods/101080  

  The **`.wabbajack`** file is the one you are looking for.

- 3 - Open `Wabbajack.exe`, then go to the **Browse** tab on the left.  
  In the top-right corner, select **Install from Disk**, navigate to the downloaded Nexus file, and choose the **`.wabbajack`** file.
  
 > (while it downloads its possible it fails due to enbseries_fallout4_v0501 not downloading, *if* the download fails head over to http://enbdev.com/mod_fallout4_v0501.htm and download it.
 > place the zip file inside the download directory wabbajack created for your modlist, restart wabbajack and continue as you did above in step 3 from here it should finish correctly.)
